Key observations:
- **Revenue beats every quarter, stock fell after five consecutive reports** (TipRanks: "fallen in each of past five earnings reports"; -20.8%, -16.3%, -18.5%, -11.4%). The drivers were never top-line: (a) wider-than-expected GAAP/adjusted losses (Q2'25 adj. -$0.27 vs -$0.21; Q1'26 -$1.40 vs -$1.17), (b) capex guidance far above Street (FY25 $20-23B; FY26 $30-35B -> $31-35B -> $35-39B), (c) soft next-quarter guides (Q1'26 guide $1.9-2.0B vs $2.29B; Q2'26 guide $2.45-2.6B vs $2.69B), (d) margin compression (adj. EBITDA margin 62% -> 61% -> 57% -> 56% -> 59%; adj. operating margin 16% in Q2'25 down to 5-6% by Q4'25-Q2'26) as interest expense exploded ($264M Q1'25 -> $640M Q2'26).
- **Q2 2026 broke the pattern** (+19.3%): a genuine beat-and-raise with a positive Q3 guide midpoint vs consensus, plus elevated short interest (15.8% of float, 2.3 days to cover) and bearish options skew made for a squeeze.
- **Options market has under-priced the move**: EarningsWatcher says actual exceeded implied in 5 of 6 reports (83%); Bloomberg data via Investing.com said 3 of first 5. Average absolute post-earnings move over the four quarters to Aug-2026 was ~16.75% (TipRanks). Implied moves have ranged 11-19%; the Q3'26 implied (~20%) is the highest pre-report figure found.
- **Guidance cut, Nov 2025**: FY25 revenue cut to $5.05-5.15B (from $5.15-5.35B) and FY25 capex to $12-14B (from $20-23B) because a third-party data-center developer was behind schedule; Intrator declined to say whether it was Core Scientific (JPM's Mark Murphy asked). FY25 capex ultimately came in at $14.9B. The developer delay plus the Oct-30 CORZ vote failure compounded a ~-16% day.
- **Backlog trajectory**: $25.9B -> $30.1B -> $55.6B -> $66.8B -> $99.4B -> $104.2B (ex >$25-29.6B signed in early Q3'26, i.e. ~$129B+ pro forma). Contracted power 2.2 GW (6/30/25) -> 2.9 GW (9/30/25); active power >850 MW (12/31/25) -> 1.5 GW (6/30/26); target >1.7 GW active by YE26.
- **Balance sheet**: cash $4.2B (YE25) -> $5.5B (6/30/26); principal debt $35.6B at 6/30/26 incl. $10.0B unsecured senior notes and $6.6B converts (StockTitan analysis). 2026 funding: $4.0B 1.75% converts (Apr), $2.6B loan facility (Aug), $4.2B 2.875% converts + 35M-share ATM (Sep). HSBC estimated an $8.9B 2026 liquidity shortfall (Jan 2026).

## 3. Cross-asset / thematic sensitivities noted by press
- Trades as a levered proxy for hyperscaler capex and Nvidia demand: fell with neoclouds on Microsoft's below-consensus capex (Oct 2026); HSBC's core thesis is over-reliance on Microsoft and Nvidia.
- OpenAI exposure: OpenAI contracts ($11.9B Mar-25, +$4B May-25, +$6.5B Sep-25) and Meta (~$14.2B, Sep-25) built the backlog; Fortune framed Q3'25 as "guidance drops amid bubble fears". TechTimes (Aug 2026) referenced "50% default odds" discourse around CoreWeave debt.
- Financing headlines move the stock as much as earnings: Sep-2026 convert/ATM announcement reversed a +7% premarket into a loss; 10/30/26 drop attributed to "financing concerns".
- Insider supply (Magnetar, founders) was a persistent overhang after Aug 2025.

## Patterns observed by journalists/analysts (with sources)

1. **"Beat and fall" / sell-the-news streak.** NVDA beat EPS estimates five quarters in a row (Aug 2025 - Aug 2026 window) yet fell after four of them; day-of reactions ran roughly +3.3%, -0.8%, -3.2%, -5.5%, -1.8% before the +8.7% break in Aug 2026. Motley Fool counted declines on the day after earnings in six of the last eight quarters before Aug 26 2026. Sources: https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/nvidia-fallen-four-straight-earnings-103457320.html ; https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/08/26/nvidia-has-fallen-after-four-straight-earnings-beats-heres-why-it-could-happen-again/ ; https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/08/25/prediction-nvda-shares-fall-5th-straight-quarter/ ; https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/21/here-we-go-again-with-nvidia-falling-on-earnings-what-the-sellers-are-missing.html
2. **No correlation between beat size and direction.** EPS beats of 8.01%, 4.00%, 3.64%, 5.32%, 5.42% across five quarters showed "no meaningful correlation" with post-earnings direction; guidance, not the print, drives the reaction. Source: Yahoo Finance / 24/7 Wall St. links above.
3. **Expectations bar / "would have had to beat by a long way."** CNBC (Feb 2026): Nvidia "would have had to beat all expectations by a long way in order to see a pop." Multiple pieces cite elevated positioning, ~30x forward P/E into May 2026, and a $4-5T market cap leaving little room for multiple expansion. Sources: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/26/nvidia-nvda-stock-price-q4-earnings.html ; https://intellectia.ai/blog/nvda-stock-earnings-analysis-may-2026
4. **Implied moves consistently larger than realized.** Reuters (Nov 2024) noted post-earnings moves "have typically undershot market expectations"; ORATS (Aug 2025/Aug 2026): implied 5.4% vs 7.4% 12-quarter average realized, "volatility expectations are falling"; Optionomics: average implied ±6.97% vs average actual 4.15%, only 1 of 7 recent reports exceeded implied; earnings-watcher: actual exceeded implied in 4 of 16 (25%), later 5 of 17 after Aug 2026. Seven of the eight reports in this dataset closed inside the implied straddle; only Aug 2026 (+8.74% vs 5.4-5.9%) broke out. Sources: https://www.tradingview.com/news/reuters.com,2024:newsml_L4N3KE17P:0-nvidia-results-could-spur-record-300-billion-swing-in-shares-options-show/ ; https://orats.com/blog/nvidia-options-280-billion-earnings-swing ; https://orats.com/blog/nvidia-earnings-options-market-expectations-vs-reality ; https://optionomics.ai/earnings-moves/NVDA ; https://earnings-watcher.com/wiki/nvda-implied-move ; https://convexvalue.com/earnings/NVDA
5. **Implied move compressing over time.** Implied straddles fell from ~8-9% (Nov 2024, Feb 2025) to ~6.5-7% (May/Nov 2025) to ~5.4-6.4% (Aug 2025, Feb/May/Aug 2026); Aug 2026 was the "lowest volatility expectation in two years" (CryptoBriefing) and Susquehanna noted the May 2026 weekly straddle (6.5%) was below the 8% average implied of the prior 10 reports. The one quarter where the market under-priced the move was the one with the lowest implied. Sources: https://cryptobriefing.com/nvidia-implied-move-lowest-volatility-two-years/ ; https://www.kiplinger.com/investing/live/nvidia-earnings-live-updates-and-commentary-may-2026
6. **Beat magnitude shrinking as scale grew, then re-widening.** Revenue beats: FQ4 FY25 +3.1%, FQ1 FY26 +1.7%, FQ2 FY26 +1.6% (smallest), FQ3 FY26 +3.8%, FQ4 FY26 +3.5%, FQ1 FY27 +3.5%, FQ2 FY27 +4.4%. Guidance-vs-consensus: +1.1%, +2.9%, -2.0% (H20), +1.7%, +5.4%, +7.4%, +4.8%, +3.6%. The two largest negative reactions (Feb 2025 -8.5%, Feb 2026 -5.5%) did NOT coincide with the smallest beats; the smallest beat (Aug 2025) produced the smallest move. Derived from CSV figures; see per-quarter sources.
7. **Nvidia tried to "reset" Street estimates.** Yahoo Finance (late 2025): "Nvidia didn't like Wall Street's estimates. So it tried to reset them." - management's $500B visibility and later "exceeding $500B" and "70% FY28 growth" commentary were framed as attempts to pull consensus up. Source: https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/article/nvidia-didnt-like-wall-streets-estimates-so-it-tried-to-reset-them-122218541.html
8. **Macro overshadowing micro.** Nov 2025 (S&P -1.6%, 3.6% intraday reversal; AI-bubble and Fed-cut angst) and Feb 2026 (Nasdaq -1.3%; AI-trade anxiety, OpenAI deal overhang) show that NVDA's post-earnings direction tracked the broad tape rather than the print. Sources: https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-11-20/us-stocks-rally-as-nvidia-earnings-ease-ai-bubble-worries ; https://www.forbes.com/sites/rscottraynovich/2025/11/20/market-reaction-to-the-nvidia-earnings-show-new-levels-of-ai-anxiety/ ; https://finance.yahoo.com/news/live/stock-market-today-sp-500-nasdaq-retreat-as-nvidia-falls-5-despite-stellar-earnings-210034836.html
9. **Post-earnings drift was negative in 4 of 5 measured quarters.** Trefis 5D/21D: Feb-25 -10.6/-15.1; May-25 +5.3/+17.0; Aug-25 -5.5/-1.9; Nov-25 -3.4/-1.5; Feb-26 -6.4/-12.4. Longer-run base rate (Chart Library, ~40 reports 2016-2026): 5-day positive ~63%, mean +4.4%. Sources: https://www.trefis.com/stock/nvda/articles/599759/how-will-nvidia-stock-react-to-its-upcoming-earnings-3/2026-05-19 ; https://fintel.io/seps/us/nvda ; https://chartlibrary.io/blog/nvda-after-earnings-history
10. **Data-center line is the swing factor.** Aug 2025 (DC in line vs Zacks $41.11B) was framed as a "miss" and the stock slipped; Nov 2025 / Feb 2026 / May 2026 DC beats of 4-5% vs StreetAccount still saw declines, showing DC beat alone is insufficient.
